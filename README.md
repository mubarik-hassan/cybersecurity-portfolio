# Cybersecurity Portfolio

Welcome to my cybersecurity portfolio!

This repository serves as a collection of independent projects, technical exercises, and research exploring various aspects of cybersecurity and information security.

## Areas of Focus

My interests span various areas of cybersecurity, with projects exploring topics such as:

- **Security Assessments & Risk Management:** Evaluating security controls, identifying vulnerabilities, assessing risks, and developing remediation strategies.
- **Security Operations & Incident Response:** Investigating security events, analyzing technical evidence, and exploring defensive security practices.
- **Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC):** Understanding security frameworks, organizational policies, regulatory requirements, and security assurance.
- **Threat Intelligence & Digital Forensics:** Examining cyber threats, analyzing digital artifacts, and developing investigative skills.
- **Cloud Security & Emerging Technologies:** Exploring security considerations associated with cloud environments, artificial intelligence, and evolving technologies.

These areas are not exhaustive, and the scope of this portfolio will continue to expand as I explore new technologies, methodologies, and cybersecurity disciplines.
